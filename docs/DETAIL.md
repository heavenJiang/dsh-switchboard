**Switchboard**: command Harness Agents from your phone through Telegram and Feishu bots — send text, photos, files, and voice notes, watch replies stream, answer approvals and the Agent's questions, and follow long tasks. The sidebar **Switchboard** entry shows conversation history; each Session's **Switchboard** tab links chats.

**Setup**: under **Lines** below, add a Telegram line (bot token from [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather)) or a Feishu line (App ID and App Secret of a self-built app with the bot capability and persistent-connection events), fill in **Allowed users** (send `/whoami` to the bot for your id) and the **Default working directory**, then **Save**. Changes apply immediately.

Full guide: [README](https://github.com/heavenJiang/dsh-switchboard/blob/main/README.md).
